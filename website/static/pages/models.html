Methods & evaluation

Conductivity models

Two models approach the same regression problem with different representations. Their weights are offline in this release; the descriptions below define what will be connected.

01 / TABULAR

HGB-SVR

Checking status…

A histogram gradient boosting model accounts for a concentration–viscosity trend and learns a correction. A support vector regressor provides a second estimate. The historical baseline combines the two in log-conductivity space.

Input representation
47 original numerical features: ion identities, solvent and anion descriptors, concentration and its derived terms, log viscosity, coordination and derived ratios.
Prediction target
log₁₀(σ + 10⁻⁶), with σ expressed in mS/cm. The original inverse transformation is required for reported conductivity.

Validation and test metrics await verification against the original fitted pipeline.

02 / MOLECULAR

SMI-TED

Checking status…

The selected partial fine-tuning model represents the electrolyte as a sequence of molecular structures and composition values. Part of the pretrained encoder is fine-tuned for conductivity prediction. Its learned molecular representation is combined with the physical features.

Input representation
A 768-dimensional molecular representation from the original tokenization and encoding pipeline, combined with the original 47 numerical features.
Required artifacts
The generic base checkpoint, selected partial fine-tuning checkpoint, tokenizer, exact formulation builder, trained fusion head and fitted numerical preprocessing.

SMI-TED performance

Validation and test metrics await verification against the original partial fine-tuning run.
